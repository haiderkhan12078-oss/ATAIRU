01 — THE REALITY

AI is moving fast.
Your operating model isn’t.

Most organizations do not have a technology problem. They have a clarity, ownership and adoption problem. The companies that win will redesign how decisions are made and work gets done.

91%

of companies do not yet have AI embedded in their strategy.

76%

of leaders say AI impact remains limited to personal productivity.

88%

of companies have no metrics connecting AI impact to P&L.

Source: ATAIRU × SAP Czech Republic, AI in Leadership Survey 2025

Ask better questions.

Where can AI change business outcomes?

Identify a process and its cost, speed or quality. Define the outcome before selecting technology.

Who owns the change?

Assign a business owner and involve the people doing the work. Agree how decisions and risks will be managed.

How will we know it worked?

Set a baseline, a target and a review date. Measure outcomes alongside adoption.
